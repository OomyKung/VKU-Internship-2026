FIM Algorithm Capability Summary
================================================================================
diffusion_model
- ic [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | default | notes=Independent Cascade with edge-specific or fallback activation probabilities.
- lt [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | notes=Linear Threshold using edge weights or normalized incoming influence.
- wc [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | notes=Weighted Cascade using inverse in-degree activation probabilities.

spread_estimator
- monte_carlo [available] | surface=search_and_final_evaluation | default | notes=Trusted final evaluator for spread, extra_spread, MF, DCV, and F-score.
- ris_guidance [available] | surface=search_guidance_only | notes=Search-time RIS guidance only; never replaces the final Monte Carlo path.
- fairness_aware_ris [available] | surface=search_guidance_only | notes=Fair-RIS weighting for search-time guidance only; final reporting remains Monte Carlo.

community_method
- louvain [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | notes=Graph-native community partition with preferred input=graph.
- leiden [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | default | deps=igraph | notes=Graph-native community partition with preferred input=graph.
- multilevel [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | deps=igraph | notes=Graph-native community partition with preferred input=graph.
- infomap [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | deps=igraph | notes=Graph-native community partition with preferred input=graph.
- label_propagation [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | notes=Graph-native community partition with preferred input=graph.
- walktrap [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | deps=igraph | notes=Graph-native community partition with preferred input=graph.

embedding_method
- deepwalk [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Random-walk based shallow embeddings using unbiased walks.
- node2vec [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Biased random-walk shallow embeddings.
- line [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=First- and second-order proximity embeddings.
- vgae [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=Variational graph autoencoder with a GCN encoder.
- gcn [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=Unsupervised graph autoencoder with a GCN encoder.
- graphsage [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | default | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=Unsupervised graph autoencoder with a GraphSAGE encoder.
- dgi [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=Deep Graph Infomax self-supervised embeddings.
- graphcl [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=Graph contrastive learning with two augmented views.
- metapath2vec [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=Heterogeneous graph embeddings over typed metapaths.

clustering_method
- kmeans [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | default | notes=Embedding-space clustering with preferred input=embedding.
- spectral [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Embedding-space clustering with preferred input=embedding.
- agglomerative [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Embedding-space clustering with preferred input=embedding.
- dbscan_or_hdbscan [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Embedding-space clustering with preferred input=embedding. HDBSCAN is unavailable, so DBSCAN fallback is used.
- gmm [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Embedding-space clustering with preferred input=embedding.

ranking_model
- random_forest [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Random-forest regressor over handcrafted node features.
- xgboost [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=xgboost | notes=Gradient-boosted tree regressor over handcrafted node features.
- mlp [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Feed-forward MLP regressor over handcrafted node features.
- logistic_regression [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Logistic classifier that ranks nodes by probability of belonging to the top-budget singleton-utility set.
- graphsage [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | default | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=GraphSAGE regressor used by the GNN ranking backend.
- gcn [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | deps=torch,torch_geometric | notes=GCN regressor used by the GNN ranking backend.

optimizer_mode
- greedy [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | notes=Spread-oriented greedy search over the trusted Monte Carlo search estimator.
- local_search [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | notes=Bounded swap local search over the shared search-time evaluator.
- evolutionary_algorithm [available] | surface=hybrid_optimizer | notes=Population-based EA component in the unified hybrid optimizer.
- swarm_intelligence [available] | surface=hybrid_optimizer | notes=Leader-guided swarm component in the unified hybrid optimizer.
- hybrid_si_ea [available] | surface=trusted_fim_pipeline | default | notes=Unified SI+EA optimizer with repair and local search.
- repair_heuristics [available] | surface=hybrid_optimizer | notes=Repair stage that keeps candidate seed sets valid and fairness-aware.

debias_mode
- none [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | default | notes=No explicit debiasing; preserves the non-debiased ML baseline mode.
- class_weighted [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Priority-class weighted GNN objective. GNN ranking backends only.
- focal_loss [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Auxiliary focal loss on top-budget priority targets. GNN ranking backends only.
- worst_group_boost [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Worst-group boosted GNN loss; the strong GraphSAGE stack uses this mode. GNN ranking backends only.
- group_dro [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Group DRO-style robust weighting for GNN training. GNN ranking backends only.
- adversarial [available] | surface=stack_pipeline | notes=Adversarial debiasing head with gradient reversal. GNN ranking backends only.
